01

Architecture

An ellipse of about 188 by 156 metres, rising about 48 metres. Travertine from the quarries near Tibur clothed a core of tuff, brick, and concrete. Eighty arches carry the Tuscan, Ionic, and Corinthian orders, and an attic of Corinthian pilasters.

02

Spectacle

The amphitheatre was built for gladiatorial munera, animal hunts, and public ceremony. Numbered arches took the crowd in, and vomitoria let it out. A canvas velarium could shade the cavea. Ancient writers describe extraordinary games at the dedication in AD 80.

03

Legacy

The building outlived the spectacles. Stone was quarried from it, and the earthquake of 1349 brought down much of the southern outer wall. With the historic center of Rome, it has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1980.
